Our thoughts always jump from an idea to another. However, most writing tools force us to document linearly: Document A, then Document B. obsidian uses internal links intensively and builds our knowledge base into a network. 

As mentioned in [[How Steph Ango uses Obsidian]], no matter if a thought is a "Daily Note", a "Post", or a "Clipping", in obsidian they are all just nodes in the graph.

Using such a system like obsidian, especially in the age of [[AI]], is good for our [[personal growth]] in the long-term.
