In the age of AI, building a personal [[knowledge base]] is more critical and feasible than ever. Its true power isn't just in *storing* information, but in *retrieving* and *connecting* it. 

Traditionally, we spend hours organizing folders and tagging notes, hoping we'll find them later. But as the database grows, maintenance becomes a chore. But now, AI can help you develop, manage, and use the content easily.

> "Your notes are not a storage unit; they are a thinking partner."

With tools like [[obsidian]] and LLMs, your static text files can become a dynamic conversation. You can ask your vault questions and discover connections you missed.
